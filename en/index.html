FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Your question isn't listed? We'll answer you personally.

Ask a question
Which certifications do your products have?
We offer developed products certified to NIJ. Solutions to VPAM, SK1 and TR are in development. Certifications are always documented per product.
How quickly can you deliver standard products?
Subject to availability and existing certification, quantities of up to approx. 100 vests are possible within around four weeks. Up to approx. 1,000 vests can be realised at short notice; larger quantities by project agreement.
How long does a custom development take?
Typically around two to three months including potential certification – depending on requirements and testing process.
Do you offer body armour specifically for women?
Yes. Our female and male fits are equal and individually designed – for complete protection and high wearing comfort.
Do you support public tenders?
Yes. We support public-sector projects with sampling, technical specification and project-specific development through to delivery.
Can existing products be adapted?
Yes. In addition to new developments, we adapt existing products quickly and pragmatically – for example fit, cut or protective build-up.
Do you also advise manufacturers and industry partners?
Yes. Our consulting covers product development, manufacturing optimisation, site search, production set-up and process optimisation – independent of any manufacturer.
